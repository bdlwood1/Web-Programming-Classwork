Luke Skywalker

Luke Skywalker Darth Vader Ahsoka Tano

Luke Skywalker is one of the most important heroes in the Star Wars story. He grows up on Tatooine with his aunt and uncle and dreams about leaving the planet to explore the galaxy. His life changes when he meets Obi-Wan Kenobi and learns about the Force and the Jedi. Luke eventually discovers that he has a strong connection to the Force and begins training to become a Jedi.

In LEGO Star Wars games and sets, Luke appears in several different outfits that represent different parts of his story. Some versions show him as a young farm boy on Tatooine, while others show him as a Rebel pilot or a Jedi Knight. He has also appeared in many LEGO Star Wars sets based on locations and battles from the movies. Because Luke changes so much throughout the Star Wars story, there are many different LEGO minifigure versions of him.

Luke is best known for using a lightsaber, learning from Jedi Masters, and helping the Rebel Alliance fight the Galactic Empire. He trains with both Obi-Wan Kenobi and Yoda as he learns how to control the Force. Luke also discovers that Darth Vader is actually his father, Anakin Skywalker. By the end of the original trilogy, Luke becomes a Jedi and helps bring his father back to the light side of the Force.

Quick Facts

  • Home world: Tatooine
  • Side: Rebel Alliance / Jedi
  • Weapon: Lightsaber
  • Famous vehicle: X-wing starfighter
